# IRD 192.168.110.10 (Upcom UC-IRD+): Eutelsat 65W, 11304 MHz

Checked with Richard on 22 Oct 2026, from the IRD's own pages and probes of
both its outputs.

- "The provider" is United Teleports, the uplink: they take channels in on
  C-band, transcode them to H.265 and uplink them to Eutelsat 65W on 3
  transponders. Faults on many channels at once can be rain at their uplink.
- Tuner: 11304 MHz, symbol rate 34000. Normal: C/N about 12 dB, Eb/N0 about
  9.5 dB, BER 0, level about -33 dBm. 35 services, about 59 Mb/s.
- In the satellite list a "$" before a name means scrambled; no "$" means clear.
- Two CI cards decrypt 8 services each.
  CI-1: BET 1840, Sony Movies 1930, Paramount 2000, TNT Series 2010,
  Travel 2020, Al Jazeera 2030, Discovery ID 2040, TLC 2070.
  CI-2: Universal TV 2080, WBTV 2090, Sony 2120, Lifetime Lat 2150,
  A&E 2160, USA 2180, Rush1 2300, Rush2 2310.
- A fault on several services of one CI card only, and none on the other
  card or on the clear ones: look at that CI card (its CAM, its subscription).
- In the clear from the satellite: TLC-Guyana 1, NCN-Guyana 2, GODTV 2100,
  Revival 2110, GEB 2260, GLTV 2330, radios 1000, 1001, 1002.
- Scrambled and not decrypted by this IRD: Univision 1660, Telemundo 2060,
  H2 2140, MS-NOW 2190, FOX Deportes 2200, Cooking 2210, Studio Universal
  2220, Cinemax 2230, Nickelodeon 2240, Comedy 2250. Our channels of these
  come from another IRD.
- Output 1: the remux (the 16 decrypted and the clear services) to
  224.100.1.5:49500, fixed at 40 Mb/s, 7 TS packets per UDP. Our channels
  read this one.
- Output 2: the whole transponder as received (scrambled ones still
  scrambled) to 224.100.100.7:33500. What uses it is not known yet.
- In the remux some names differ from the satellite's: TLC is "TLCL", A&E is
  "AE-LAT", NCN radio is "NCN-RADIO".
- Program 1650 in the remux is an empty leftover (no name, no data): a "no
  data" on it is not a fault.
- GLTV 2330 is about 145 kb/s even from the satellite (a still or slate):
  low but normal.
- On 22 Oct the remux had stale tables for TLC-Guyana and NCN-Guyana (wrong
  PIDs: TLC had no sound and NCN's video, NCN had no video); removing and
  re-adding them in the remux fixed it. If a service of the remux has
  streams that do not match the satellite's, its remux entry is the first
  thing to rebuild. GEB and GLTV were added to the remux that day.
- The remux makes its own tables (PAT/PMT): when a service's content stops
  on the way in, its tables keep arriving. A "no data" with the tables still
  there points at the satellite, the uplink or this IRD, not the network.
