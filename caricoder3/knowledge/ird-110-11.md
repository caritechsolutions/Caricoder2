- Its tuner is on the same transponder as 192.168.110.10 (11304 MHz, the
  same 35 services). Normal: C/N about 12.5 dB, Eb/N0 about 10 dB, BER 0,
  level about -30 dBm. Both IRDs' C/N falling together: the dish, the LNB,
  rain or the satellite; only one: that IRD or its cable.
- 192.168.110.10's raw tuner output (224.100.100.7:33500) stands for this
  IRD's satellite too (the same transponder).

  2200, Cooking 2210, Studio Universal 2220, Cinemax 2230, Nickelodeon 2240,
  Comedy 2250 (Sony is decrypted by 192.168.110.10's CI-2 as well).

  Our nickelodeon264, comedy264, cinemax264, studiouniv264, msnbc264 read it.
- CI-2's source is "IP In": the MPTS 224.2.2.2:2000 from 192.168.110.15
  (27 services of two other transponders). CI-2 decrypts 8 of them: WSVN-FOX
  910, HBO Pop 1250, Lifetime Movies 1280, AMC Latin 1750, VH1 1770, AXN
  1790, BBC 1810, MTV 1830. Output 2 is CI-2's output, the whole of it, to

  scrambled: nobody decrypts them. Their "scrambled" is not a fault.
- A fault on 224.2.2.3 only, with 224.2.2.2 fine: this IRD's CI-2 (its CAM).
  On both: 192.168.110.15 or its satellite. The C/N that matters for
  224.2.2.3 is 192.168.110.15's, not this IRD's tuner.
- On 22 Oct its remux differed from the satellite: Nickelodeon and Comedy
  had gained Spanish tracks (2243, 2253) not in the remux; Studio Universal

  the remux entries (and re-select them on CI-1) to clear it.

  satellite: labels are not to be trusted; listening decides.
