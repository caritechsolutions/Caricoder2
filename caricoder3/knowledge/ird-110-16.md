# IRD 192.168.110.16 (Multi-Channel CI-Receiver): one tuner in use

Checked with Richard on 22-23 Oct 2026, from its web interface (cgi.php,
password admin/admin) and its Output page. The same make as 192.168.110.15.

- Tuner 1 only (caricoder3 reads it as mcr://192.168.110.16?tuners=1).
  Tuner 2 has no signal (about -95 dBm) and is not used: not a fault.
- Tuner 1: transport stream id 9, the same transponder as 110.15's tuner 1.
  Normal: C/N about 12.25 dB, level about -28 dBm, BER 0. One of the two
  low and not the other: that IRD's cable, connector or tuner, not the dish.
- Up since the breaker trip of 21 Oct; 67.8 °C with the room's air
  conditioning off (its normal with it on is not known yet).
- Card 1 decrypts ANIMP, CARTOONITO, CNNUS, WPLG-ABC, WFOR-CBS, DISC-SCI,
  DISC-TURBO, FOXS2; card 2 WSVN-FOX, WPBT-PBS, WTVJ-NBC, HGTVE,
  1160-CARTN, FOXSOC, DISNEY, NICKJR.
- Its MPTS output is off. It sends 15 single-program outputs, all to
  224.2.2.2, on the even ports 3002 to 3030 (about 1.5-1.9 Mb/s each, 3 Mb/s
  cap): CNNUS 3002, ANIMP 3004, 1160-CARTN 3006, CARTOONITO 3008, DISC-SCI
  3010, DISC-TURBO 3012, FOXS2 3014, WPLG-ABC 3016, WFOR-CBS 3018, WSVN-FOX
  3020, WTVJ-NBC 3022, HGTVE 3024, FOXSOC 3026, DISNEY 3028, NICKJR 3030.
  SPTS-16 is off.
- WPBT-PBS is decrypted but not output here: PBS is carried elsewhere (not
  a fault).
- 224.2.2.2 is also the group of 110.15's MPTS (port 2000): a receiver that
  joins it for one of these gets all of that group's traffic.
